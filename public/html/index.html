TechMapsPro

TechMaps Pro is a web platform built to provide students with a complete learning environment for mastering technical skills.

While researching online learning, I found that the completion rate of many self-paced online courses is only around 40–50%, even though thousands of free and paid resources are available. The real problem isn't the lack of content—it's the lack of structure, guidance, and consistency.

That's why I built TechMaps Pro, where every roadmap combines a structured learning path with curated videos, notes, progress tracking, and daily guidance, helping students stay consistent and complete their learning journey.

SchemeNavigator

SchemeNavigator is a web application that helps students discover government schemes they are eligible for through a personalized recommendation system.

Finding the right government scheme is often confusing because eligibility criteria, required documents, benefits, and application processes are scattered across multiple government websites. Many students either miss opportunities or spend a lot of time searching for reliable information.

To solve this, SchemeNavigator collects a student's academic and personal details, matches them with relevant government schemes, and provides complete information including eligibility criteria, required documents, benefits, application process, and official application links—all in one place.
